Privacy

Generative Palette A11y processes palettes and sketch frames locally in your browser. We do not upload, receive, store, or sell your images, colours, or exported notes.

No tracking

This site includes no advertising trackers, analytics scripts, account system, or third-party runtime fonts. Your browser may request the static page files from the host in the ordinary course of loading a website.

Your exports

When you choose “Export field notes,” a JSON file is created directly by your browser and saved where you choose. We never receive it.
