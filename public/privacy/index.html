Privacy and local processing

Generative Palette A11y processes palettes and sketch frames in your browser. It does not upload, receive, or sell your images, colors, or exported notes.

Network requests

This site loads its own page files. It includes no advertising tracker, analytics script, account system, or third-party runtime font.

Sample data

The sample demo may save its sample palette under a separate demo: browser key. Resetting the demo or choosing “Start for real” removes that key. The demo never writes to a real study.

Your exports

Choosing “Export field notes” creates a JSON file in your browser. You choose where to save it. The product never receives that file.
