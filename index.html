How it works

Use the report in three steps

  1. Load colors and frames. Add your palette and moments from the actual sketch loop.
  2. Inspect the highest-risk pair. Review simulated separation and visible area together.
  3. Try a bounded alternative. Apply a lightness change, then export field notes for the sketch.

Limits and privacy

What this report can and cannot tell you

Color vision varies within and beyond named categories. This tool uses simple sRGB matrix approximations for three common differences.

It cannot predict every person’s perception or every display condition. Use flagged pairs as places to inspect, not as certification.

Images stay in your browser. The sample demo uses a separate browser key and never writes to a real study.
