Read before publishing

A useful approximation, not a certificate.

Colour vision varies within and beyond named categories. This lab uses simple sRGB matrix approximations for protanopia, deuteranopia, and tritanopia, then compares simulated colour separation and relative luminance. It cannot predict every person’s perception or the effects of context, displays, motion, texture, and size.

Frame weighting assigns image pixels to their closest supplied swatch, so it is most informative when the palette is concise and the frames are representative. Treat flagged pairings as places to inspect, not a universal pass/fail.
